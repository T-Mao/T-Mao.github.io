Tongze Mao

I am a software engineer in test at Veeva Systems, working on Vault infrastructure across the Durable Queue Framework, FileManager and File Staging API, and the Configuration Framework. My work includes release validation and test automation, maintaining queue automation and triage pipelines, upgrade testing, and defect investigation. I also raise design concerns with developers when proposed behavior leaves gaps in reliability or testability.

Before joining Veeva, I spent two years developing apps in Swift and Flutter. I published eight Swift apps on the App Store through my independent studio, TZAppify, and built two cross-platform Flutter apps for SupTech. One of my iOS apps reached the Top 20 in Productivity during March, April, and May 2025.

I graduated Cum Laude from UC Irvine with a double major in Computer Science and Informatics, and a minor in Statistics. I also completed a research internship at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, working on data preparation for endoscopic 3D reconstruction.

My interest in software started with using it. I have purchased more than 160 apps, especially from independent developers, and enjoy studying how they solve specific problems. Building my own products led me to detailed test plans and automated testing. Finding a bug, understanding its cause, and testing the fix remain some of my favorite parts of the work.
