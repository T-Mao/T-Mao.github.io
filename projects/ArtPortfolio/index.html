Art & Creative Work

Exhibited artwork, official event graphics, original WeChat stickers, handmade objects, writing, and music.



A creative practice alongside engineering

My creative work spans drawing, graphic design, character illustration, handmade objects, and writing. The Future City (2017) was selected among 100 works from 10,000 entries in an international competition and exhibited at Lincoln Center in New York. Arcadia High School selected my design as the official poster for its 3rd Annual Art Exhibition. My original WeChat stickers have also been sent thousands of times in everyday conversations.

I studied introductory and advanced graphic design at Arcadia High School, working with Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, and Animate alongside drawing and painting. The collection below includes exhibition work, public event materials, published character collections, and practical objects I designed for my own home.

Handmade objects for everyday use

On October 20, 2026, I designed and made a collection of small objects with air drying clay. I started with two everyday inconveniences: shopping bags without a convenient place to hang, and nail clippers and other small tools scattered across my desk. I shaped a hook for an existing rail and a divided holder for the tools, alongside several small vase forms.

A place for shopping bags

I wanted a place to hang my shopping bags where I could reach them easily. The hook’s upper curve rests over a metal rail on my shelf, while its lower curve holds the bag handles. The bags now hang beside the shelf, ready to pick up when I need them.

Nail clippers, within reach

Loose nail clippers and other small grooming tools were cluttering my desk. I made an open organizer with separate compartments for clippers, scissors, and files. The tools stand upright and remain visible, with enough exposed to grasp. Each compartment gives them a place to return to after use.

Small vessels, different silhouettes

I also made small vases with rounded and folded forms. The two shown here use the same decorative stems, making their different openings, proportions, and silhouettes easier to compare.

Drawing and painting

Still Life: Pear (2024)

A pencil study of a pear, drawn from life. I concentrated on the transition from highlight to shadow, using changes in value to describe the rounded fruit and distinguish it from the cast shadow.

Tibet: Digital Collage (2018)

My digital collage of Tibet was selected for Arcadia High School’s annual art exhibition, which presented student work to the wider community. I combined photographs and graphic layers in Photoshop and Illustrator, bringing landscapes, architecture, and cultural details into one composition.

The Future City (2017)

In August 2017, The Future City was selected among the top 100 of 10,000 entries in the Future Art China International Competition. The competition asked entrants to depict an imagined city of the future.

My painting was among 1,000 semifinalist works exhibited in Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Hunan Province. It then advanced to the final 100 works exhibited at Lincoln Center in New York City. The painting also appeared in media coverage and was reproduced in a photobook of the competition’s finalists.

I spent a week researching future architecture and environmental design, followed by about a month painting. I imagined elevated districts linked by bridges, with waterfalls and vegetation sharing space with dense architecture. I combined brushwork and spray painting to develop the city and its surrounding landscape.

Male Portrait Study (2017)

A graphite profile study focused on the relationships between the forehead, nose, lips, and chin. I used changes in value to describe the face and neck instead of relying on a strong outline everywhere.

Female Portrait Study (2017)

A companion graphite portrait with a different pose and softer transitions across the face. I worked on the angle of the head, the placement of the features, and the contrast between hair and skin.

Hand Gesture Studies (2017)

These sketches examine hands from several angles and in different poses. I built each drawing from larger shapes before adding fingers, knuckles, and shadows, keeping the overall gesture legible.

Skull Studies (2017)

Three views of the skull, comparing the eye sockets, cheekbones, and jaw from different angles. I used the changes in plane to describe the structure beneath a portrait.

Plaster Cast Studies (2017)

Studies of an eye, a nose, a bust of Voltaire, and an anatomical head cast. I identified the major planes first, then used transitions in value to describe their depth and the direction of the light.

Early Studies: Still Life and Figure (2014)

Still life, cast, and figure studies from 2014, focusing on simple shapes, observed proportions, and shading.

Graphic design and print

Annual Art Exhibition: Official Poster (2020)

Arcadia High School selected my design as the official poster for its 3rd Annual Art Exhibition. The exhibition presented selected work from across the student body to the wider community, spanning Art Honors, screen printing, photography, and animation. My poster represented the event as a whole and was used to promote it on campus and beyond.

I created the poster in Photoshop and Illustrator, using a prominent “Art” title, contrasting geometric forms, and patterns to bring the exhibition’s different disciplines into one composition. A separate information panel keeps the event details clear.

Orchesis Dance Company: Recruitment Poster (2020)

I was selected to design Orchesis Dance Company’s poster for its 2020 to 2021 auditions. I researched the company’s visual style and culture, developed the layout independently, and worked directly with its leadership on feedback and revisions. The design organizes auditions, workshops, meetings, and eligibility requirements into a clear reading order. The company adopted the finished flyer and displayed it prominently inside and outside its building.

Dance Day: Two-Sided Flyer (2020)

The front presents Dance Day 2020 through a bright palette and a prominent title. The reverse carries the registration form. Designing both sides meant switching between promotion and utility: one side invites someone to participate, and the other gives them a clear way to sign up. I worked on hierarchy, typography, and a print-ready layout.

Speech Follies: Event Program (2020)

A cover and interior spread for the 2020 event program. I organized schedules, names, and event information through a consistent typographic hierarchy and carried a related visual language across the booklet. Compared with a poster, the program demanded more attention to sustained readability and the relationships between multiple pages.

Social Science: Classroom Poster Series (2019)

A four-part series about cognitive, communication, historical-thinking, and passion-inspired skills. I used a related layout and color system to make the pieces belong together, while giving each topic its own emphasis. The posters were used in school classrooms and display areas. The task was to organize several statements without losing the larger message.

Questions for Grades 6 to 9 (2019)

An educational poster built around question words and ideas such as change, shape, and perspective. I used varied lettering and a playful palette to give the text movement while retaining a clear grouping. The design was made for a younger school audience, which required a different tone from the more formal classroom series.

Air Pollution Awareness Poster (2019)

A public-awareness composition combining a person, industrial imagery, and a direct call to stop air pollution. I worked with photo editing and contrasting visual layers to connect an environmental issue with a human subject. The challenge was to create an immediate focal point while keeping the message readable.

Speech Follies: Event Poster (2019)

Arcadia High School selected my design in its Speech and Debate team poster competition. The poster promoted the team’s Speech Follies event on campus and beyond.

More than 100 copies were printed and displayed in every classroom, outside teaching buildings, and on walls throughout the school. Team members also carried a large-format version around campus and into classrooms to introduce the event in person.

I developed the design in Photoshop and Illustrator over a month. The finished composition places a speaker among spotlights, audience silhouettes, and scattered punctuation, with the title arranged vertically.

Georgia O’Keeffe: Bookmark and Invitation (2019)

A MoMA-inspired exhibition design exercise, pairing a bookmark with a cut-and-fold invitation. I used a shared type system and layout to connect the two formats, then arranged the invitation’s imagery around the way it opens and folds.

ULike: Logo Exploration (2019)

Six logo directions for a fictional bath-products brand, created in an advanced graphic design course. The alternatives compare different relationships between lettering and symbols, from compact marks to more expressive wordmarks.

Clean Campus: Environmental Sticker (2019)

A campus campaign sticker featuring a superhero and the message “Let’s Pick It Up, Apaches!” I used a strong silhouette, a short line of text, and a self-contained shape to make the environmental reminder recognizable at sticker size.

Inspirational Quote Poster (2018)

A typography and image composition built around the idea of doing one’s best today, selected for school display. I emphasized the final word through scale, color, and a contrasting image treatment.

P!nk: Music Poster (2018)

A personal music poster featuring P!nk, combining a photographic portrait with expressive lettering and layered color. Music has long been one of my interests, and this piece let me work with a more energetic visual voice. I focused on keeping the performer recognizable while using the surrounding type to set the mood.

Orchesis: Hip-Hop Class Flyer (2018)

A flyer for Orchesis Dance Company’s open hip-hop class, using a diagonal layout and expressive typography. I organized the event information around the company’s promotional requirements and revised the design accordingly.

Principles of Design (2018)

Eight small compositions demonstrating balance, contrast, emphasis, movement, pattern, rhythm, unity, and variety. Shapes, repetition, and color make each principle visible within a shared page layout.

Digital illustration and stickers

The monochrome drawings use posture, facial expression, and short lines of text to carry reactions and jokes at conversation size, while keeping the character recognizable across different moods.

Writing and music

Chinese prose and fiction

I write original short pieces in Chinese, including classical prose, and have published my work online. One of my classical Chinese pieces earned fourth place in a writing competition.

I am also developing a novel, exchanging drafts and feedback with writer friends. I especially enjoy writing stories with unexpected turns.

Singing and musicals

Singing is one of my favorite ways to unwind. I am an enthusiastic musical fan, with favorites including Hamilton, EPIC: The Musical, SIX, and Elisabeth.

What I carry into product design

These projects involve both design and delivery: researching a competition brief, preparing graphics for public events, publishing original characters, and making objects I use at home. The finished piece has a job to do, whether that is helping someone find event details, express a reaction, or put a tool back where it belongs.
