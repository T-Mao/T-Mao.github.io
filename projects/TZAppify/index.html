TZAppify

My independent app practice: product decisions, native iOS development, and release ownership.

A small studio with a personal point of view

TZAppify is the name I use for my independent app work. I design and build tools for everyday activities: planning a day, keeping a habit, studying a deck of cards, choosing a typeface, or writing to a future version of yourself.

I have taken more than ten app projects from idea to working product, including eight Swift apps published on the App Store and two cross-platform Flutter apps for my previous employer. One of my iOS apps reached the Top 20 in Productivity during March, April, and May 2025.

Those releases began with a much quieter habit: collecting apps. I have purchased more than 160, especially from independent developers. I pay attention to how an app introduces its main action, handles a mistake, or makes a complicated task feel manageable. Building my own apps is how I put that curiosity into practice.

The work behind each release

For my independent apps, I own the product scope, interface, Swift and SwiftUI implementation, local data handling, testing, and App Store submission. Working alone means I see how each decision affects the rest of the product. Adding an interaction also means deciding how it should persist, how it can be undone, and how it behaves when the user returns tomorrow.

My apps share a preference for direct interaction. DoneTodo lets people paint time onto a grid. Flipperly turns review into a flip and a swipe. FontMate connects a visual choice with a code snippet. The interface should make the purpose of the app tangible.

What keeps me building

I enjoy the full process, but debugging has become a particular interest. Designing a test plan makes me examine a product more closely: what happens after an accidental tap, after relaunching, or on the boundary between two dates? I began studying automated testing for my own apps and carried that interest into my work as a software engineer in test.

My goal is to make small apps with a clear purpose and give their details enough care that people can rely on them.
